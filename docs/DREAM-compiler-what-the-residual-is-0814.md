## 3 · ⭐⭐ THE STRUCTURAL DIAGNOSIS

**All four ratified classes are CLAIM-SHAPED.** *type-traps = a formal OBJECT; misattributed =
a CAUSE; stale-citations = a REFERENT; wrong-scope = a measured SENTENCE.* **Every one asks
"what is wrong with this assertion about an artifact?"**

⇒ ***THE CORPUS ALSO CONTAINS PROCESS-SHAPED DEFECTS: obligations dropped at a handover,
allocations duplicated, budgets overrun, assertions never checked, channels that leak, custody
methods that risk the record.*** **A claim-shaped taxonomy cannot see a process-shaped defect,
and 7–9 of 28 residual rows are exactly that.**
