## ⭐ RESULT: THREE OF THE FOUR COMPONENTS ARE ALREADY RATIFIED. ONE IS NOT.

The deferred question was *"what does coverage COUNT?"* — asked as one question. Reading the
codebook, it is **four** questions, and three have ruled answers already on the record.

| component | status | ratified text |
|---|---|---|
| **THE UNIT** — what gets counted | ✅ **RULED** | **§1.5** *"THE CODED UNIT IS THE POST, and the precedence runs over ALL FINDINGS IN IT."* |
| **THE DERIVATION** — counted from what | ✅ **RULED** | **§4.2(c)** *"WHERE BOTH WRITTEN REASONS ARE PARAPHRASE WITH NO QUOTED TEXT … **the row must be re-read from `FLEET.md` before it is filed**."* |
| **THE CLASSIFICATION** — counted as what | ✅ **SETTLED BY THE WITHDRAWAL** | `:88` reason (c): DRAFT 1's figure *"measures SIDES of a disputed pair, and pass-3 coders pick CLASSES, not sides."* |
| **THE DENOMINATOR** — over what population | ⛔ **GENUINELY ABSENT** | nothing on the record fixes it |

## What this means

**No new rule is needed for three quarters of the question**, which is the same shape as the
SEAM-A ruling: the text already decides it. A B-2 coverage figure counts **POSTS**, classified
by **CLASS**, derived from **ROWS RE-READ IN `FLEET.md`**.

⚠️ **AND §4.2(c) IS STRONGER THAN THE WITHDRAWAL'S OWN SUMMARY OF IT.** The withdrawal note at
`:88` says §4.2(c) *"forbids filing those from reasons alone."* The clause does more than
forbid — **it prescribes the remedy**: the row must be re-read from `FLEET.md`. *That is
precisely what the B-1 gate sweep did, so the sweep is not merely compatible with the
re-derivation the codebook demands as a BLOCKING pre-ratification condition — it IS that
re-derivation, for the 289 rows it covers.*

## ⛔ THE PART THAT IS ACTUALLY OPEN, now much smaller than "define the unit"

**The DENOMINATOR.** Coverage of what, over what? Candidates visible in the record, none ruled:

- the 289 pass-1 `OTHER` rows (the B-1 sweep population), or
- all 278 double-coded rows (DRAFT 1's population, whose figure was withdrawn), or
- the full corpus of posts.

*These differ, and a figure computed over one and labelled with another is exactly the
`a-count-is-not-a-scope` failure the withdrawal's four reasons were written to prevent.*

⇒ **STEP (b) IS THEREFORE NARROWER THAN IT LOOKED: not "define the coverage unit" but "fix
the denominator and name it in every figure."** That is a one-line stipulation, and it can be
refuter-passed cheaply — which was the whole point of reading first.

## What is NOT claimed here

Nothing is proposed and nothing is ruled. The denominator is **not** chosen here — naming the
candidates is not picking one, and picking one is the helm's act, not mine. No coverage figure
is computed anywhere in this document.
