THE THREE AXES, AS PINNED BY THE RULING (not my choices — recorded so a reader
can check I implemented what was ruled rather than what I preferred):
  SCOPE  DESIGN outputs only — the `outs` prefix.
         PREDICATE: table.length == <name>_ndesign_out, NEVER outs.length.
         (Fabric: outs 76, design 24 — a declared-outputs table can never match
         outs.length on any sequential or cut datum.)
         State and cut entries stay deliberately UNNAMED: flop D nets and cut
         roots are not ports, and their pairing is already recorded by position
         and the flop table's Q column.
         TRIPWIRE so the unnamed tail stays guarded:
           outs.length == table.length + nstate + ncut  is ALSO asserted.
  UNIT   one entry = one EMITTED BIT. C-V1 forces bit-expanded callers, so a
         32-bit rdata contributes 32 rows.
  ORDER  the caller's --outputs order, RECORDED. That order IS the datum's index
         semantics; recording it is what makes outs[k] checkable. There is no
         importer-side order to derive (:604). The guarantee is VALIDATION, not
         derivation: no entry lands that is not C-V3-checked against the
         declared set.
