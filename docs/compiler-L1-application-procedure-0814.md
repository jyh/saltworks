### 2.1 ⭐ WHY FOUR MARKS AND NOT ONE — THE ONE DESIGN DECISION THAT MATTERS
**A ladder violation requires writing a contradiction.** *To file `69485` at T4 under this
form I must write `T2 FIRE <span>` and then `filed T4` on the next line.* **AMBIG becomes
unwritable when a test is marked FIRE.** ⇒ ***THE FORM DOES NOT ASK ME TO BE MORE CAREFUL. IT
MAKES MY MEASURED FAILURES REQUIRE A VISIBLE SELF-CONTRADICTION*** — my own banked law that a
form which automates the claim beats one that reminds you to make it.

📌 **And it prices honestly: four marks per finding is ~4× the writing of a single class
call.** *That cost is the deliverable, not a side effect. A procedure that were cheaper would
be the one both passes already ran.*
